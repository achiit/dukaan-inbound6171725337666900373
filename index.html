Behind Glow & Grace

Designed for Real Pune Weather, Not Just Photo Shoots

I started Glow & Grace in Pune back in 2025 after getting tired of watching my favorite gold chains turn dark after just a few weeks of wear. I remember my sister gifting me a delicate gold pendant for my birthday, and within a month of daily college wear and Pune monsoons, it had lost its luster.

I realized working women and students in Pune need accessories that can handle humidity, sweat, and daily commute without costing thousands. That is why every single piece in our studio is hand-selected, 18K gold-plated over hypoallergenic steel, and tested for real life.
